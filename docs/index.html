Dental Practice Software: How Big Is the Market, and Where to Launch First

TAM / SAM / SOM for per-office practice software, built from Census County Business Patterns (2018 and 2023) and a public price list. Every number below is computed by the Java model in the repo.

Offices by employee count, 2018 vs 2023

Small offices are shrinking while 10+ employee offices grow, which is the group-practice / DSO consolidation story showing up in Census data.

TAM and SAM by price scenario

TAM = every dental office x 12 months x price. SAM = offices with 5 to 49 employees only.

Launch-state ranking

Base weights: 40% target offices, 20% five-year growth in target offices, 20% payroll per office (ability to pay), 20% target offices per 100k residents (route density). Each criterion is a percentile rank across 50 states + DC. Top 10 highlighted.

SOM: first-three-year ARR in the 10 launch states

Share captured is a planning assumption (1%, 2%, 5%), shown as a grid instead of a single number.

Data: U.S. Census Bureau, County Business Patterns 2018 and 2023 (NAICS 621210); Census Vintage 2025 population estimates; Open Dental published fees. Code: github.com/hrutishaa/DentalMarketSizing
